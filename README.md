# 🚀 MESH WARS: Frontier Traders

## ▶️ [Play now: kq4cin.github.io/mesh-wars](https://kq4cin.github.io/mesh-wars/)

Click the link above. The game runs in your web browser. There is nothing to download and no account to create.

---

## What is it?

A space game where you captain a starship. You can:

- 💰 **Trade** goods between planets to earn credits
- 🗺️ **Explore** a galaxy of 34 star systems
- ⚔️ **Fight** pirates in turn-based battles using dice
- 👩‍🚀 **Hire crew**: humans, aliens, and robots
- 🔧 **Upgrade** your ship with new weapons and gear
- 📡 **Meet other players** over a MeshCore radio (optional)

---

## First time playing?

1. Open the [game link](https://kq4cin.github.io/mesh-wars/).
2. Type a name for your captain and click **Launch career**.
3. A built-in tutorial called **Flight School** will walk you through everything.

You can restart the tutorial anytime with the **? GUIDE** button at the top of the screen (just **?** on phones).

The game works on phones and tablets in both portrait and landscape. On a phone, the tabs move to the bottom of the screen (portrait) or the left edge (landscape), and every panel fits the screen width, so there is no sideways scrolling.

## Install it like an app (optional)

Installing puts an icon on your home screen and lets you play **without internet**.

| Device | How to install |
|---|---|
| **Android** (Chrome) | Open the game → tap the **⋮** menu → **Install app** or **Add to Home screen** |
| **iPhone / iPad** (Safari) | Open the game → tap the **Share** button → **Add to Home Screen** |
| **Windows / Mac** (Chrome or Edge) | Open the game → click the **install icon** in the address bar |

## Is my game saved?

Yes. Your game saves automatically in your browser.

- It is saved **on that device only**.
- Clearing your browser data will erase your save.

## Playing with other people (optional)

You do **not** need this to play. The game includes pretend players so the galaxy feels alive.

To play with real people nearby, you need a **MeshCore companion radio** (a small LoRa radio device).

1. Use **Chrome** or **Edge** on a computer or Android phone.
2. In the game, open the **Mesh** tab.
3. Click **USB Serial** or **Bluetooth LE** and pick your radio.

The game automatically sets up a public channel on your radio called **#meshwars**. Other players on that channel show up in **Captains on the Mesh**.

To talk to them, tap **✉ Hail** next to a captain (or **Hail all**) and pick a canned message such as *"Want to trade? Meet me at …"* or *"Mayday!"*. Replies show up in **Comms // Hails** with one-tap quick replies. Hails are tiny packets so they don't clog the mesh; a hail written while you're off-grid is sent when your radio connects, or dropped after 30 minutes.

> ℹ️ Radio connections do not work in Safari or on iPhone.

## For developers

The entire game is one file: [`index.html`](index.html). It uses no libraries, images, or fonts. All graphics and sounds are generated by code.

To run it on your own computer:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/kq4cin/mesh-wars.git
cd mesh-wars
python -m http.server 8000
```

Then open <http://localhost:8000> in your browser.

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The whole game |
| `manifest.webmanifest` | Lets the game be installed as an app |
| `sw.js` | Lets the game work offline |
| `icons/` | App icons |
